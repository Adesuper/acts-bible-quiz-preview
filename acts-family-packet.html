How to use this packet

  1. One page a day, Monday to Friday. Each page is one official study day — about 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. Read the passage out loud. This is the single most important habit. Reading aloud helps children remember far more than reading silently.
  3. Read it a second time and hunt for the numbers, names, and places. Those are where quiz questions come from.
  4. Answer the questions on the page. It is fine to look back at the passage.
  5. Say it back. Close the book and ask your child to tell the story in 30 seconds.
  6. Saturday is the live quiz with the whole team. Sunday is a rest day.

There are 74 study days across 15 weeks, covering every verse of Acts 1–16.
